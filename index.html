Her friends were driving to Melbourne for their hair. So she started here.

Some hairdressers qualify without any training in cutting or styling afro hair. After immigrating to Ballarat, Natacha Adanlessossi saw the people she knew making the trip to Melbourne, and began offering her skills locally.

Her manner is warm and consultative. Her eye takes in the texture of your hair, its porosity, length, density and potential. Her hands do the rest, so you leave delighted, proud and positive.

The only Australian hairstylist to receive an Australian Multicultural Foundation Carla Zampatti Certificate of Distinction.

At a multicultural celebration at Lake Wendouree, she created 15 free hairstyles in one day for women of many cultural backgrounds.
